Full-Stack Ad-Ops & Attribution Tracking Pipeline

Server-side GTM + GA4 + Meta CAPI pipeline turning raw click traffic into verified ROAS telemetry.

The Problem / Bottleneck

Client-side tracking was suffering from 25-40% signal loss due to ad-blockers, Safari ITP cookie expiration, and iOS privacy controls, causing degraded ad optimization and blind ad spend.

The Architecture & Solution

Architected a dual-delivery tracking pipeline: client dataLayer feeds a custom Server-Side GTM proxy on a custom subdomain. Event data is deduplicated with unique Event IDs and dispatched directly to Meta Conversions API and GA4 Measurement Protocol, backed by Looker Studio reports.

Rust Compile-Time Zero-Overhead Portfolio Engine

Maud macro pre-renderer producing a single-pass ~20KB payload with zero initial JS execution.

The Architecture & Solution
