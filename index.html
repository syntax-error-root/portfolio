Server-Side Tracking & Ad-Ops Telemetry Pipeline

Custom server-side GTM proxy and event broker preserving conversion telemetry through Safari ITP and ad-blocker signal loss.

The Problem / Bottleneck

Client-side tracking scripts were losing up to 35% of ad attribution and revenue telemetry due to aggressive ad-blockers, iOS privacy sandboxing, and Safari 7-day cookie expiration limits, distorting ROAS calculations.

The Architecture & Solution
